	<h2 class="mb-4">The Evolution of the Golf Ball</h2>
	<p>Golf balls have come a long way since the 14th century. The first golf balls were made of wood, followed by the "featherie" — a leather pouch stuffed with boiled goose feathers. As materials and technology evolved, the ball transformed dramatically.</p>

	<h4 class="mt-4">Historic Milestones</h4>
	<ul>
    		<li><strong>Wooden Balls (1300s–1600s):</strong> Carved from hardwood, mostly beech. Extremely inconsistent in flight.</li>
    		<li><strong>Featherie Balls (1600s–1800s):</strong> Leather pouch packed with feathers. Expensive, hand-crafted, and fragile.</li>
    		<li><strong>Gutta-Percha Balls (1850s):</strong> Made from rubber-like sap. More durable and cheaper to mass produce.</li>
    		<li><strong>Rubber Core Balls (1900s):</strong> Featured a solid or wound rubber core with outer balata or surlyn cover.</li>
    		<li><strong>Modern Multilayer Balls:</strong> Today’s balls often have 2–5 layers for optimized spin, distance, and feel.</li>
  	</ul>
